ABL Announces Four New Franchises for Season Three
January 2018 · ABL League Office · Official Statement

The AI Ball League has officially announced that Season 3 will feature four new expansion franchises, bringing the total number of ABL Premier teams to twelve. The announcement was made by the league office ahead of the Season 2 opener, confirming that the expansion process is already underway.

Twelve teams. The ABL is growing — and it's growing fast.

The four new franchises will join the league in 2019, competing in the ABL Premier alongside the eight existing teams. The league confirmed that the expansion cities have been selected but will not be revealed until prior to Week 3 of Season 2. Ownership groups for all four franchises have already been finalized.

The move doubles down on the league's founding ambition: build something that scales. Season 1 proved four teams could sustain a compelling competition. Season 2 proved the format holds at eight. Season 3 will test whether twelve teams and a full expansion era can deliver the same quality of play that has defined the ABL from the start.

Roster construction, draft eligibility, and scheduling format for the twelve-team season will be announced in the coming weeks. The league office stated that the competitive structure will evolve to accommodate the expanded field, with more details expected around the Week 3 city reveal.
