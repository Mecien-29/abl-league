Second EP Game in ABL History The first was NHG vs LAF in Season 1 Week 5 — settled in EP1. This one needed EP2. Both P8 and EP1 ended in ties, forcing a second sudden-death period. FLV wins it 5–4 in EP2, with Sanchez ending on a double Recovery — the second such sequence in league history.
🌀
Coolly's P8 Seven-Pointer Nearly Won It LAF trailed 13–8 entering P8. Coolly's seven-point period tied it 15–15 and forced EP. Then EP1 tied again at 18–18. Coolly scored 4 in EP2 but Sanchez answered with 5. One of the most dramatic games in ABL history.
📊
Sanchez 2-1 · FLV Rotation Still Unsettled Sanchez wins again as starter — both FLV victories have come with him starting. Hodge is 0-3. The numbers are speaking loudly at this point.
