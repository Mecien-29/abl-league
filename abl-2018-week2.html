// Official · Season 3 Expansion Reveal · Before Week 3
Meet the Four. The ABL's Next Chapter Has a Name.
2018 · ABL League Office · Prior to Week 3 · Official Statement

The AI Ball League has officially unveiled the four franchises joining the ABL Premier for Season 3 in 2019. The cities were confirmed earlier this season. Today, they have names, colors, and identities. The league is twelve teams deep. There is no going back.

San Diego. San Francisco. Atlanta. New Orleans. The ABL goes coast to coast.

The San Diego Tide arrive from the Pacific coast wearing deep navy and sea foam — built around the relentless, inevitable energy of the ocean. The San Francisco Cipher bring something no other ABL franchise has: a tech-coded identity in charcoal and neon green, a franchise that looks like it was designed in a server room at 3am.

From the South, the Atlanta Blaze come in scarlet and burnt gold — fire, heat, a city that has rebuilt itself more than once and burns brighter every time. And completing the four, the New Orleans Specter in midnight purple and Mardi Gras gold. Haunting. Atmospheric. Unlike anything else in this league.

Four cities. Four identities. One season to prove they belong.

Roster construction for all four franchises is underway. Draft eligibility rules, scheduling format for the twelve-team season, and the full Season 3 fixture list will be confirmed at the conclusion of Season 2. Team logos will be unveiled separately ahead of the Season 3 launch.

// Transfer Watch
Wills Goes Solo. Wall Finds a Partner.
Pierre Wills leaves New Hampshire to build something in Arizona — alone, on his own terms. John Wall, who ran the entirety of Season 1 without a teammate, finally has one: Pete Watts in Colorado. Two of the most interesting stories of the offseason.

// Rivalry Watch
Wilson and Watson — The New York–Boston Thread
Jack Wilson leaves Boston for New York Circuit, where he'll team with rookie Alex Watson. Meanwhile Sam Watson (no relation) anchors Los Angeles. The Wilson-Watson name game across three cities is already a story. When NYC meets LAF, it'll be worth watching.
