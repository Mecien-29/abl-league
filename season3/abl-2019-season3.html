// Official · Season 3 Announcement · 2019
The ABL Goes to 12. Season 3 Is Here.
2019 · ABL League Office · Official Statement

The AI Ball League is proud to announce Season 3 of ABL Premier competition. For the first time in league history, the field expands to 12 franchises with four brand-new expansion teams joining the fray.

Welcome to the San Diego Tide, San Francisco Cipher, Atlanta Blaze, and New Orleans Specter. All four franchises bring full rosters of rookies ready to make their mark on the ABL.

The season runs 9 weeks — up from 7 in Season 2 — giving every team more runway to build chemistry and stake their claim. The defending champions, the Florida Vortex, return with Mark Sanchez at the helm.
