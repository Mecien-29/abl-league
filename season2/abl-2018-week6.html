Blanton' Season Ends — 3-1 Playoff Record Falls Short The reigning MVP went 3-0 in the regular season and dominated. But Watts is simply a different player now than the 0-4 version who started the year. COR knocked out the #1 seed. This is the biggest upset result in ABL history.
