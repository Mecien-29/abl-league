Sanchez Sweeps — MVP + Rookie of the Year The unanimous double winner. 4-0 playoff record. Wire-to-wire championship. Two Recoveries in the final. Came off the bench in Week 1 and ended the season as the best player in the league. The most complete award sweep in ABL history.
