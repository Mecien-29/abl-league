// Stat Definitions
STAT GLOSSARY
G
Games played. Any appearance in a game counts — starter or relief.
GS
Games started. Player was on court for Period 1 of the game.
RA
Relief appearances. Player entered in P2 or later. Max one per half — first half P1–P4, second half P5–P8.
PTS
Total points scored across all appearances. Corners and Super Saves included.
PA
Points allowed. Opponent's score during the periods this player was active.
PPG
Points per game. PTS ÷ G. Minimum 2 games for leaderboard eligibility.
PRA
Points Ratio Against. PA ÷ PTS. Lower is better. The primary efficiency metric of the ABL. <1.000 = outscoring opponents.
W / L
Wins and losses credited to the player active when the decisive period ended. Starting players hold the record unless substituted.
