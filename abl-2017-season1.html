MEET THE EIGHT: ABL REVEALS IDENTITIES OF ALL FOUR EXPANSION FRANCHISES

The ABL Premier has a name for every city. Months after announcing that Arizona, Colorado, Florida, and New York would join the league for the 2018 season, the league office has now revealed the official team identities for all four expansion franchises — and the names carry weight.

ARIZONA MESA

The desert southwest gets a name rooted in the land itself. A mesa is unmovable — a flat-topped formation of rock that has outlasted every force nature has thrown at it. The Arizona franchise chose the name deliberately. In a league built on momentum and explosive scoring, they want to be the team that doesn't bend. The organization cited the geography of the region as foundational to the identity: ancient, resilient, and impossible to erode. Mesa isn't flashy. It's permanent.

COLORADO RIDGE

High altitude. Sharp edges. Colorado's franchise name evokes the ridgelines of the Rocky Mountains — jagged, precise, and unforgiving. The ownership group wanted a name that captured what competing at elevation feels like: every movement deliberate, every decision carrying more consequence. Ridge also speaks to the way the team intends to play — technical, calculated, hard to get past. The mountain doesn't move around you. You have to go over it.

FLORIDA VORTEX

Nobody in the league announcement room was surprised when Florida came in swinging. Vortex was chosen because it describes exactly what the ownership group intends to build — a force that pulls everything toward it and destroys it at the center. Florida's climate produces some of the most violent atmospheric events on the planet, and the franchise wants that same unpredictable, overwhelming energy on the floor. Sources close to the organization describe an aggressive roster-building philosophy to match the name. Consider the rest of the league warned.

NEW YORK CIRCUIT

New York doesn't sleep, and neither does a circuit. The city's franchise took its name from the electrical grid that powers the most wired metropolis on earth — a system that is always on, always running, always connected. Circuit also speaks to speed. Information moves through a circuit without friction. New York's ownership wanted a name that felt modern, urban, and relentless. In a league where reaction time and flow state define performance, there's no better metaphor than electricity moving through a closed loop at full voltage.

All four franchises are expected to announce rosters and player signings ahead of the 2018 season opener. The ABL Premier is no longer a founding experiment. It's a growing league with eight franchises, four markets hungry for their first title, and a whole new set of rivalries waiting to be written.
